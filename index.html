GreenKrest - Animation Samples

5 animation styles for the GreenKrest website. The page design is kept simple and the same in all 5, so you can judge only the animation.

Tap any sample to open it. Watch the hero, the numbers and the product cards, that is where each animation style shows itself.
Animation 1

Marquee Motion

A delivery truck drives across the hero, category names scroll in a moving band, numbers count up and cards slide in. Full of movement and energy.

 Animation 2

Float and Glow

Product pictures float gently in the air, a soft green glow pulses behind the headline and the cards breathe with a glowing shadow. Calm and premium.

 Animation 3

Draw and Build

A girder drawing draws itself line by line, bars fill up under the numbers and the cards build up one by one. Technical and satisfying.

 Animation 4

Bounce and Play

A small crane swings a slab, the order button bounces softly and the cards bob and pop when touched. Friendly and playful.

 Animation 5

Flow and Steps

A wave flows under the hero, customer lines change smoothly and the order process lights up step by step: Order, Confirm, Dispatch, Delivered. Smooth and corporate.
