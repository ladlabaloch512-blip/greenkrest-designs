FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers before you order. For anything else, call us any time.

How do I place an order?
Tap any Order on WhatsApp button or call us on 0370 9595953. Tell us the product, quantity and your site location. We confirm the total and delivery day before anything moves.
Which areas do you deliver to?
We deliver across Rawalpindi and Gujar Khan, and to nearby areas on request. Delivery charges depend on your area and the size of the load. We always tell you the full amount before dispatch.
Can I get a custom size in slabs or girders?
Yes. We stock common sizes and can guide you on sizes for your plan. Share your room or roof sizes on WhatsApp and our team will suggest the right sizes and quantities.
How do I pay?
You check the material when it reaches your site, then pay. For large orders we may ask for a small booking amount first, and we always share the receipt details with you.
What if a piece arrives broken?
Point it out at delivery time. Broken pieces from loading or transport are replaced or adjusted in your bill. That is our promise on every order.
